# Tailliuir Studio

Welcome to Tailliuir Studio — the family's home for streaming addons and network management.

## 🚀 Overview

Tailliuir Studio is designed to keep your streaming setup clean, organized, and running smoothly. 

- **Core Addon:** You need the **Tailliuir Network** addon installed to connect to the studio. Everything else (status checks, guides, and support tiles) builds on top of it.
- **Backend:** Powered by TorBox for reliable caching and direct debrid streaming.
- **Quality Guide:** Streams are colour-coded like a traffic light:
  - 🟢 **Green:** Best link available
  - 🟠 **Orange:** Okay link
  - 🔴 **Red:** Avoid or use only as a last resort
- **Screen Resolution:** Not on a 4K screen? Simply skip links tagged with **4K** when picking a stream.

---

## 💡 Frequently Asked Questions

- **Q: Do I need anything else installed?** A: Just the Tailliuir Network addon to connect you to the Studio; the rest is optional.
- **Q: My TV isn't 4K, what do I do?** A: Skip any link tagged 4K when choosing a stream — everything else works fine.
- **Q: What do the coloured dots on streams mean?** A: Green = best link, orange = okay, red = avoid or last resort. This only applies to Tailliuir Network.
- **Q: Something's not working. What should I check?** A: Check `status.torbox.app` first — most issues are a TorBox outage, not your setup.
